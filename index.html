How I got here

Air ForceStudyIndustryLife

  1. 2012 · Israeli Air Force

    Israeli Air Force

    In 2012 I was serving in a software unit of the Israeli Air Force, with about a thousand developers and no designers. Most people there saw UX as graphic design.

  2. 2012-2017 · HIT

    B.Sc. in Technology Management

    Alongside my service I studied for a B.Sc. in Technology Management at HIT, from 2012 to 2017.

  3. Key milestone

    2015 · Israeli Air Force

    Founded the UX wing

    I taught myself UX and pitched it. Told no, I started anyway, as a volunteer. My work on the aerial defense software during operation "Tzuk Eitan" improved operator response times and won a unit commendation.

    In 2015 my role became official. I ran the IDF's first UX conference and founded the Air Force UX wing, with funding in the millions.

  4. 2017-2018 · Webiks

    Webiks

    I was the only designer on two Air Force projects: a maintenance app for technicians, and a management and simulation system for large-scale military drills. I did the research on the bases myself: interviews with experts and field technicians, base visits, and field validation of mockups.

  5. 2018-2019

    Twenty countries

    Then I took a year off and travelled through twenty countries.

  6. 2020 · Asperii

    Asperii

    One of the projects I worked on was a clinic management system for a top US healthcare enterprise, covering treatment plans, schedules and resource allocation across thousands of US clinics and hundreds more internationally.

  7. Key milestone

    December 2020 · Asperii

    Promoted to team lead

    Four months after joining. I hired and trained a design team of three on the Salesforce platform.

  8. Key milestone

    2022-2026 · Salesforce

    Salesforce

    Only designer on ten AI product areas in Service Cloud, working with six engineering teams in India, Israel and San Francisco, and four product managers.

    Among them: real-time case classification, AI email replies and record companions.

  9. Personal milestone

    2024

    Became a mother

    I live in Tel Aviv with my family, a dog and a cat.

  10. 2026

    Next stop
