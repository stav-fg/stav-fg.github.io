What I'm best at

Going deep

I learn the domain before I design it: interviews with experts and field technicians, visits to Air Force bases, a study of how clinics run. Then I work through the details, like a separate error message for each cause of failure.

Designing AI features

Training a model and choosing which one runs, drafting text for a person to approve, letting AI act on its own, and deciding what it's allowed to touch. How I approach it.

Prototyping with AI

I build working prototypes on the design system, with AI-assisted tools and Figma-to-code conversion, and hand them to engineering in place of static Figma files.

Working with engineering

I design within real constraints and work with engineers to balance what the design needs against what can be built: the Salesforce component library at Asperii, the limits of a hardened tablet at Webiks, and the capacity of six engineering teams at Salesforce.
