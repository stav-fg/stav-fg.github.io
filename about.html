About

Get to know me

I'm Stav, a product design lead in Tel Aviv. I've spent 14 years designing software for people who use it under pressure, from Air Force operators to support teams at Salesforce.

How I got here

2010-2012 · IDF

IDF

I did my army service in the IDF, first in project management and then in training and standards.

2012 · Israeli Air Force

Israeli Air Force

In 2012 I was serving in a software unit of the Israeli Air Force, with about a thousand developers and no designers. Most people there saw UX as graphic design. I taught myself the practice and pitched a UX function to the head of the unit. The answer was no, so I started anyway, as a volunteer after hours, under a title I gave myself. Within months it was about 80% of my time.

2012-2017 · HIT

B.Sc. in Technology Management

Alongside my service I studied for a B.Sc. in Technology Management at HIT, from 2012 to 2017.

2015 · Israeli Air Force

Founded the UX wing

The clearest result of those years was the aerial defense support software, which operators in a field command centre used to track incoming threats. Operator response times improved during the 2014 operation, and the project received a unit commendation. Looking back, those screens needed much more whitespace. In 2015 my role became official. I ran the first UX conference in the IDF and founded the Air Force UX wing, with funding in the millions to staff it.

2017-2018 · Webiks

Webiks

At Webiks I was the only designer on a maintenance app for Air Force technicians, and I did the research on the bases myself.

2018-2019

Twenty countries

Then I took a year off and travelled through twenty countries.

2020 · Asperii

Asperii

At Asperii I designed a clinic management system for a top US healthcare enterprise.

December 2020 · Asperii

Team lead

Four months in I was promoted to team lead. I hired and trained a design team of three.

2022-2026 · Salesforce

Salesforce
