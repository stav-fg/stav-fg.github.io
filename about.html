About

Product design lead with 14 years in complex software

I design software for people who use it under pressure: support reps and admins at Salesforce, and Air Force technicians and operators before that. I've led design projects and teams, and I've built a design function where there wasn't one.

What I do

Design AI features

Four years at Salesforce as the only designer on ten AI product areas in Service Cloud, working with six engineering teams in India, Israel and San Francisco. My work there covered training a model and choosing which one runs, drafting text for a person to approve, letting AI act on its own, and deciding what it's allowed to touch.

Build design teams

I was the first UX designer in the Israeli Air Force, first as a volunteer and three years later officially. I founded the Air Force UX wing and secured funding in the millions to staff it. At Asperii I built a design team of three.

Prototype in code

My last projects at Salesforce went to engineering as working prototypes built on the design system, in place of Figma files.

Accessibility

I owned accessibility for my product area at Salesforce.
