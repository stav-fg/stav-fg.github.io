## The strategic change

The build plan proposed manufacturing evidence of AI fluency by rebuilding an old design as a
working prototype. That evidence already exists and is stronger, because it is real employer work
rather than a portfolio exercise.

Her prototypes repo replaced static Figma handoff with interactive LWC builds on the internal
design system, and it is configured to be operated by coding agents: `CLAUDE.md`, `AGENTS.md`,
authored agent skills, and an MCP server wired to Figma-to-LWC conversion.

**Recommendation:** this becomes the spine of the portfolio, not a footnote. Either as a fifth
case study about changing how her team ships design, or woven through the two Salesforce studies
as the method behind both. It proves two of the three positioning claims at once, and it needs no
interview to establish that it exists.

It also cannot be shown directly. It is employer source with internal hostnames and usernames. The
portfolio piece is a sanitised rebuild of one flow with invented data, plus the written account.
