Eight things only you can do

Everything else on this project is handled. The site is built and live, the case studies are chosen, the writing is underway. These eight are the ones that need you specifically.

Nothing here should take more than half an hour. Most of it is already written for you and waiting in the shared doc.

The order is the point. The first three involve other people, so they get slower the longer they wait. The rest can happen whenever.

First, because other people are involved · 3

Update your LinkedIn copy is written Headline, About and every role description are drafted in the shared doc. Read it, cut anything that doesn't sound like you, paste it in. It's the page recruiters actually open, and right now it says you lead the design team at Asperii.
Ask a few colleagues for an intro and a recommendation email is written One email does both. The people around you now are the ones most likely to place you, and they get much harder to reach once you've left.
Ask the PMs for numbers same email Adoption, acceptance rate, edit rate. Rough is fine. Three numbers across the whole portfolio is plenty, and people take days to reply.

Half an hour in Figma · 2

Export about six frames each from RTC, A3 Registry and KGER One hero shot, a few states, any before and after pair. PNG at 2x. Where you have a choice, pick the frame with demo data over one showing a real customer. The full list is in the doc.
Send Ehud the RTC, KGER and A3 Registry Setup files Those three didn't come across with the rest, and they're three of the four case studies. Skip this one if you've exported the frames above.

Twenty minutes, typing into the doc · 1

Answer the eleven questions no document can answer These are the ones that make the case studies good. Short answers are fine, and a sentence each is genuinely enough.
  • RTC what was the July meeting feedback that changed the flow, twice?
  • KGER why is the reasoning behind a second click instead of shown by default?
  • A3 what did legal object to, and what changed because of it?
  • MSJ how do you get an org to adopt something it isn't required to?
  • and the big one: what changed in how you design because the material is probabilistic?

Five minutes each, whenever · 2

Confirm four facts for the CV Your Webiks title, which phone number is right, whether Asperii ended 2021 or 2022, and which AI tools you actually use. All four currently contradict each other somewhere.
Fix four things on your live Carbonmade site It's public right now and one page still has the template's placeholder text on it. Listed in the doc.
